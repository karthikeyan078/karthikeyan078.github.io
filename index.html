About

Building worlds that respond.

Hello! I'm Karthikeyan R V (Karthi), a game developer and Unreal Engine Gameplay Programmer with 1+ year of professional experience developing gameplay systems, VR applications, and real-time interactive experiences using Unreal Engine 5, C++, and Blueprint Visual Scripting.

I've delivered 6+ major projects, including 3 VR applications, and developed 15+ scalable gameplay systems covering gameplay mechanics, AI behavior, UI, VR interaction, character controllers, state machines, and real-time visualization.

I'm passionate about game development, VR, visual storytelling, 3D development, and story-driven games. I enjoy solving complex gameplay challenges and building systems that are responsive, immersive, and production-ready. My goal is to contribute to AAA-quality games through robust gameplay systems and engaging player experiences.
